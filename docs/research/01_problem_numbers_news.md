### A4. Law, policy and news timing

- **Supreme Court, Jan 2025 (Ajay Malik):** the Court found a "legal vacuum" and asked ministries to form an expert committee on a law for domestic workers. Business Standard, 29 Jan 2025: https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/sc-asks-centre-to-set-up-panel-to-safeguard-domestic-workers-rights-125012901056_1.html - SNIPPET
- **Centre's committee said no new law, Dec 2025:** "the Committee has concluded that introducing a new legal framework is not desirable". It says domestic workers "are already covered under the four labour codes". The report was obtained by RTI and has not been made public. The Tribune, 12 Dec 2025: https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/delhi/despite-sc-call-for-statutory-protections-panel-backs-status-quo-on-domestic-workers-rights - **VERIFIED**
- **Scroll, 9 Apr 2026:** "Why India needs a separate law for domestic workers even though the Centre is opposed to it". The committee said "a separate legislation is not needed". https://scroll.in/article/1091295/scroll_in - **VERIFIED**
- **Supreme Court, 29 Jan 2026:** declined a PIL by domestic workers' unions (Penn Thozhilalargal Sangam v. Union of India) seeking a legal framework and minimum wages. It said the solution must come from the legislature. https://supremetoday.ai/supreme-court-dismisses-pil-for-domestic-help-wages-20260130004 - SNIPPET
- **ILO Convention 189:** India voted for it in 2011 but **has not ratified** it. ILO India page, 12 Jun 2026, "Fifteen years advancing decent work for domestic workers in India": the ILO "hopes to move closer towards its ratification in India"; "domestic workers have largely received lower wages than other recognized categories of unorganized workers." https://www.ilo.org/resource/article/fifteen-years-advancing-decent-work-domestic-workers-india - **VERIFIED**
- **Telangana promised its own law, Jun 2026:** Labour Minister Dr Vivek Venkatswamy said the state will introduce a **Telangana Domestic Workers Welfare Act** for "social security, dignified wages and safe working conditions for domestic workers", with a special committee. Siasat, 16 Jun 2026: https://www.siasat.com/telangana-govt-to-soon-introduce-domestic-workers-welfare-act-3490478/ - **VERIFIED**
  - **Status by 9 Nov 2026:** I found no report of a draft, a tabled bill or a committee notification after June 2026. The law is still promised, not passed. That is the news hook: the promise is five months old.
- **Union demands:** GUTS (Gruhakarmikula Union Telangana State) memorandum of 16 Jun 2026 asks for a legal framework, ESI/PF, ID cards, a tripartite committee, and "1 per cent of municipal tax revenue" for welfare. CatholicConnect, 17 Jun 2026: https://catholicconnect.in/news/telangana-domestic-workers-seek-legal-recognition-welfare-protection-on-international-domestic-workers-day - **VERIFIED**. Earlier GUTS call (14 Mar 2026) to table the "Telangana Domestic Workers Bill 2026": link in A1. **VERIFIED**
- **Unions to name in the demo:** GUTS (Gruhakarmikula Union Telangana State, 14,000+ members), Telangana Domestic Workers Union (TDWU), National Platform for Domestic Workers (NPDW), National Domestic Workers Movement (NDWM), and Montfort Social Institute (NGO, Hyderabad).
- **Four Labour Codes in force from 21 Nov 2025; first anniversary 21 Nov 2026.** ThePrint: https://theprint.in/india/labour-codes-kick-in-after-6-yrs-minimum-wage-social-security-advance-notice-for-strikes/2789550 - SNIPPET. Central Rules notified 8 May 2026 (L&E Global: https://leglobal.law/2026/05/28/india-indian-ministry-of-labour-and-employment-notifies-central-rules-and-appropriate-authorities-under-the-labour-codes/) - SNIPPET.
  - **Why it matters:** the Centre says domestic workers are "already covered" by these codes, and the anniversary falls 11 days after the deadline.
- **Code on Wages claim mechanics (product hook):**
  - A wage claim can be filed **within 3 years** (up from 6 months under the Minimum Wages Act).
  - It can be filed by the employee, a registered trade union, or the Inspector-cum-Facilitator.
  - The authority can award compensation **up to 10 times** the claim.
  - Mondaq / LKS summaries: https://www.lkslaw.com/insights/articles/code-on-wages-2019-an-overview - SNIPPET.
  - **Caveat:** whether a single household counts as an "establishment/employer" under the Code for domestic workers is legally contested. Check with a lawyer before the demo claims a statutory route. A demand letter plus a union referral is the safe framing.
- **Telangana minimum wage overhaul:** G.O.Ms.No.6, 30 May 2026, effective 1 Jun 2026. ThePrint called it the first comprehensive revision in 12+ years. Telangana HC gave the state a "final chance" on the delay (Siasat: https://www.siasat.com/hc-gives-telangana-final-chance-to-explain-minimum-wage-delay-3518887/). ThePrint: https://theprint.in/india/cm-reddy-announces-revised-minimum-wages-for-1-11-crore-workers-in-telangana/2937515/ - SNIPPET. The ILO also welcomed it: https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/ilo-welcomes-telangana%E2%80%99s-first-comprehensive-minimum-wage-fixation-state - SNIPPET.
