ORDER: 
 
In the circumstances reported by the Commissioner of Labour in the letter 
first read above and after examining the recommendations of the Telangana 
State Minimum Wages Advisory Board on the revision of minimum wages, the 
matter has been referred to the Cabinet Sub-Committee constituted to study 
and to recommend the quantum of wages to be fixed vide G.O. 2nd read above 
and submitted revised recommendations vide its minutes fourth read above.  
 
2.     The Government after careful examination of the matter hereby agree with 
the recommendations made by the Cabinet Sub-Committee and orders to 
revision/fixation of the basic minimum rates of wages per month, in respect of 
employees in all the establishments defined under the Code on Wages, 2019, in 
supersession of all earlier notifications issued under the Minimum Wages Act, 
1948 (Central Act No.11 of 1948), in the State of Telangana, with effect from 1st 
June, 2026, subject to outcome of pending W.P’s, and CCs before the Hon’ble 
High Court for the state of Telangana or any other court cases pending in the 
matter. 
 
3. 
Accordingly, the following notification shall be published in the 
Extraordinary Telangana State Gazette, dated 01.06.2026.

(c) Cost of Living allowance: 
The Minimum basic rates of wages fixed are linked to the Consumer Price 
Index numbers at 443 points (Base year 2001 = 100 series). The 
Commissioner of Labour, Telangana, shall notify the cost of living 
allowance for every 6 months i.e 1st April and 1st October. For this purpose 
the average rise in the state Industrial Workers Consumer Price Index / 
Agricultural Workers Consumer Price Index numbers for half year ending 
December and June respectively shall be taken into account. For every rise 
of one point over and above 443 points, an increase of Rs.33.86 per point 
per month shall be paid as variable dearness allowance.

Conditions for compliance: 
1) While calculating, per day wages, the monthly wages shall be divided by 
26 days, which includes the rest day wages. 
 
2) Trainee shall be paid 75% of the wages applicable to the category, but it 
shall not be less than the Minimum Wages for an unskilled category of 
worker. 
 
3) Gender Neutrality & Pay Equality: Employers shall pay uniform and equal 
minimum wage rates to male, female, transgender, and physically 
challenged employees who perform identical or equivalent work. 
 
4) In all categories of employments where the existing notified wages are 
higher than the above recommended wages, an additional enhancement 
of 10% over the existing wages shall be provided so as to protect existing 
wage advantages available to workers.

5) Holiday and Rest Day Remuneration: Workers required to perform duties 
on designated weekly rest days or public holidays shall be compensated 
at double (two times) the normal wage rate, subject to applicable labour 
rules. 
 
6) Fractional Rounding: In final calculations of pay, fractional amounts shall 
be rounded off to the nearest 50 paise. 
 
7) Direct Electronic Disbursement: Employers must directly deposit workers' 
wages 
either 
via 
bank 
check 
or 
direct 
electronic 
transfer 
(NEFT/RTGS/IMPS) into the employee's designated bank account. 
 
8) Where the nature of work is the same, no discrimination on the payment 
of minimum rates of wages should be made in respect of male and female 
workers. 
 
9) Overtime Compensation: Any work executed beyond the standard 8 hours 
a day shall be compensated as overtime at double (two times) the 
standard rate of wages. 
 
10) Where any category of employee is actually in receipt of higher rate of 
wages than those notified above shall continue to be paid such higher 
wages. 
 
11) In case of piece rated employee, the wages paid for 8-hour work shall not 
be less than the minimum wages fixed herein in the relevant skill 
category and piece rate shall be proportionately calculated. 
 
12) Where any of the above categories of workmen are engaged/ employed 
through a contractor or service provider, the occupier/the principal 
employer shall be responsible for ensuring the payment of the minimum 
rates of wages by the contractor or service provider.
